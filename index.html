Our Dedication To Perfection

Pristine Hygiene, Master Cooks & Courteous Hospitality

At Starlight Anya, we believe true luxury starts where no diner ever compromises: immaculate cleanliness, authentic ingredients, and heartfelt care.

Uncompromising Hygiene & Cleanliness

Our kitchen follows hospital-grade sanitation protocols. All cutlery and glassware go through high-temperature automated washing followed by UV-C sterilization chambers. Our kitchen is glass-fronted so guests can view the culinary theatre in full transparency.

Certified Master Chefs

Led by veteran culinary masters with decades of 5-star hotel expertise. Whether it is slow-cooking royal Chittagong Kala Bhuna, hand-tossing 48-hour fermented Neapolitan pizza, or carving USDA Prime Wagyu beef, every plate is prepared with passionate precision.

Gentlemanly & Attentive Waitstaff

Our service team is trained in royal hospitality etiquette. Well-groomed, respectful, and observant without intruding, our servers anticipate your every need—from pairing mocktails to dietary adjustments.

Catering 6:00 AM to 2:00 AM
