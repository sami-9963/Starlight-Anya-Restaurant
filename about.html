Our Hygiene & Cleanliness Protocols

Every plate served at Starlight Anya in Saidnagar, Gulshan undergoes stringent safety scrutiny. Here is how we guarantee complete peace of mind.

UV-C Cutlery Sterilization

All forks, spoons, knives, and wine glasses are put through an 85°C thermal rinse followed by sealed UV-C chambers to eradicate 99.99% of pathogens before sealing in individual protective covers.

100% Medical-Grade Sterilization

Transparent Open Kitchen

We have nothing to hide. Our glass-paneled kitchen allows you to witness the stainless steel cleanliness, chef gloves, hairnets, and organized food stations as your meals are cooked live.

Transparent Culinary Theatre

Cold-Chain Traceability

From Padma River Hilsa fish to imported USDA beef cuts and Italian cheeses, all perishables are stored in independent temperature-calibrated refrigerators to prevent cross-contamination.

Zero Preservatives or Additives
